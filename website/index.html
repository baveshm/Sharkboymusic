01 / About

Energy that
moves the room.

Xavier Tan, known as DJ Sharkboy, is a professional DJ, MC, and event host based in Singapore. His 6 years of open format DJing span pop, progressive house, techno, and hardstyle.

Beyond the decks, Sharkboy owns every segment of your event, including intimate dinner sets, VIP introductions, full-crowd raves, and corporate galas. His ability to read a room makes him as effective in a boardroom as he is in a club.
